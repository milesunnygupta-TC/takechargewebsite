Take Charge

Why throwing exercises at a painful joint keeps failing — and what to do instead.

Most fitness plans hand you a workout schedule on day one. We don't. If your knee, hip, or lower back has been hurting for months or years, throwing exercises at it isn't strength training — it's a gamble. This sequence exists because we've seen that gamble fail too many times before it ever gets a chance to work.

What the free training covers

  • 1

    Mobility assessment & correction

    How to find out why your body has been talking to you — which joints have lost range, which movements are compensating, and where the actual problem is versus where the pain shows up. Correction comes before anything else.

  • 2

    Foundational strength

    Once the joint moves the way it should, how to build the strength that protects it — slowly, deliberately, without shortcuts. This is the phase most programs skip entirely.

  • 3

    Progressive overload — done safely

    Only now do you chase real strength numbers. A joint that can handle load is a joint that can actually get stronger — not one more injury waiting to happen.

  • 4

    How the coaching is delivered

    A real mobility screening before you begin, video-based correction where we watch how you move, and progress tracked against your data — not logged and forgotten.
