Questions, answered honestly.

Is this for weight loss or joint pain?

Both start the same way — with an assessment. Weight loss without fixing mobility first usually just adds new strain to old problems. We correct the joint, then build toward whichever goal matters most to you.

Do I need equipment or a gym membership?

No. Training is designed around what you have at home, with clear guidance on the few basics that help if you choose to add them.

I've tried physio before and it didn't work. How is this different?

Physio treats an injury. Take Charge builds the strength that prevents the next one — through an ongoing, coached sequence, not a short course of sessions.

I'm not in Pune — can I still do this?

Yes. Most of our coaching already happens over WhatsApp, Zoom, and voice notes — this works the same whether you're across town or across the country.
